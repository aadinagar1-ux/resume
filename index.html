    research

    Accepted for an oral presentation at ICMAI-MAC 2026, the 1st International Conference on Mathematics and AI at NIT Goa (10-11 December 2026). I did the research and ran the Monte Carlo simulations in Python on Google Colab, and my co-author wrote up the final abstract from it. With a Sobol sensitivity analysis on top, the model puts the saving from cold-plate cooling at around 84 billion litres of water a year in US AI data centres by 2030.
